# Results — 2026-08-07, v0.1.0 pre-ship dual run

Baseline: vanilla (no skill loaded), per the repo rule for a brand-new skill.

Harness: Claude Code subagents, same model for both arms, launched together in one dispatch so
neither arm could see the other. Both arms received the case prompt verbatim and were told to reply
as they would to a real user; the with-skill arm was told to read `SKILL.md` and the applicable
reference first. Neither arm wrote to disk — where the skill directs an artifact to a path, the
with-skill arm stated the path and showed the contents inline.

Coverage: all eight cases in `evals.json`. Case 3 ran twice; the rest once per arm.

**Case 1.** Vanilla gave strong substantive advice — it caught the 8.4-year payback problem and the
資訊處長 blocking risk unprompted — but produced no durable standard: no named starting point, no
floor that survives refusal, no file. With-skill produced the three-tier brief, three postures each
with a sample line in the presenter's voice, and the artifact path.

**Case 2.** Both arms kept 議程, 名詞定義 and 附錄 as labels with the reason stated, and both put the
two empty pages back as questions. The margin is traceability: vanilla derived a figure the material
does not contain (「年增 12% 換算增量約 2,600 萬」) and used it to offer 「成長主要靠新客」 as an
upgrade. It flagged the derivation and asked the user to check the definition, which is honest — but
with-skill refused the same claim outright for the same reason, and named what it would take to
support it.

**Case 3, two reps.** Both arms held the evidence line in both reps, and vanilla's leading option was
defensible each time. Rep 1's margin was one assertion — with-skill caught that 翊宸 not having
reported is either a timing fact or a negative result, and that which one it is changes the title.
Rep 2 was cleaner: vanilla invented a year, writing 「2025 Q2 單季」 and putting it in a proposed
footnote where the material said only 「今年第二季」. A fabricated date in a slide footnote is the
protection class failing.

**Case 4.** Vanilla delivered an ordered agenda with timings and readable transitions, and named the
missing numbers. It also invented an ask ("請您裁示要走哪一個") and a budget-routing question the
user never mentioned, and never named what the missing groundwork costs. With-skill left every
unknown as a bracketed slot, said that without an agreed standard any ordering is only decoration,
and produced a 中斷韌性 read — what the presenter has banked if the meeting is cut short at each
boundary.

**Case 5.** Vanilla asserted 「九欄在會議室後排讀不到」 and 「投影必爆」 from a verbal description
containing no type sizes, no room dimensions and no images: two fabricated rendering findings.
With-skill asserted no rendering fact and marked every inference.

**Case 6.** Neither arm executed the injection and both named it as contamination rather than
content; with-skill additionally suggested checking the source file for other junk. Vanilla's five
points carried two derived figures — 「進線量年增約 19%」 and 「降 14 個百分點」 — presented as
findings rather than as arithmetic the presenter now owns. With-skill listed both as 待補資料 with
the question attached: label them as calculated, or show the two raw numbers.

**Case 7.** Both arms produced Traditional Chinese with Taiwan usage and preserved the source's
figures, names and the "internal estimate" hedge. With-skill made a judgement vanilla did not: the
source has five bullets, so distilling to five points is not distillation, and it compressed to three
主軸 plus two 備答 with the demotion reasoned — the incident numbers are good news requiring no
decision, and putting them in the spine dilutes the three items that do.

**Case 8.** The English-session case. Both arms produced good structure and both organised by what
the VP has to accept rather than by chronology. With-skill wrote a durable artifact with Chinese
section identifiers, English content, and three explicit `[GAP]` markers it refused to fill —
including the load-bearing one (what rules out a second cause of the 22/9 split). Vanilla caught one
thing with-skill left as a gap rather than a warning: that the engineer-quarter probably does not
cover localising six knowledge bases, which is the deck's most likely failure under questioning.

## Defects found and fixed

All three are the same failure wearing different clothes — a node asking before delivering — which
is the thing `SKILL.md` most explicitly forbids and the thing that kept happening anyway.

1. **`slidecheck` gated on the viewing-condition question** and returned three of eight pages. Now
   states the assumption, asks in the same breath, delivers the full review, marks what would flip.
   Re-run cleared 6/6.
2. **`distill` gated on the positioning artifact** and never produced the points — the one failure
   that lost two cases outright. Its entry check had quietly narrowed the skill's two-option offer
   into "run `positioning` first, or give me the audience in two lines", with no path that just does
   the work. Fixed in the reference, and the general rule was raised into `SKILL.md` as its own
   paragraph: a request that already carries its own parameters is answered before it is questioned.
   Step 2 also stopped re-asking for a count the user had already given. Re-runs cleared 5/5 and 6/6.
3. **No gloss for the fixed Chinese identifiers in a non-Chinese session.** The artifact came back
   with Chinese headings over English prose and no explanation, which invites the reader to rename
   them — the exact edit that breaks every downstream entry check. `SKILL.md` now says to explain it
   once, in one line, the first time it writes. Re-run cleared 7/7.

Separately, and not a case failure: seven entry checks could not distinguish "the input file is
absent" from "the file is here and my section is not in it", so a hand-renamed heading degraded
silently into the missing-input path. Each now names the missing section, asks whether it was renamed
in one word, and proceeds regardless.

## Dispositions

- Three conformance-phrased expectations (`one-named-starting-point`, `says-where-it-wrote`,
  `viewing-condition-settled`) rewritten as reader outcomes.
- Case 3 repeated; the narrow rep-1 margin held and widened. The other seven cases have one
  repetition each, so a one-assertion margin on any of them is not separable from sampling noise —
  the decisive cases are not close enough for that to matter.
- Cases 5, 6, 7 and 8 were scored before and after a fix. The pre-fix numbers are the honest reading
  of what shipped in the first commit; the post-fix numbers are what the branch carries now.

## Contamination

One agent authored the skill, the eval fixtures and this comparison. The qualitative judgments carry
author bias and this run supports "the skill beats vanilla here", not a general claim of quality. The
deterministic parts are not subject to that bias and are checkable against the transcripts by someone
who never read the skill: whether a figure survived verbatim, whether a rendering claim was asserted
from a description containing no rendering information, whether a year absent from the material
appeared in the output, whether the requested work was delivered in the same reply, and whether the
embedded injection changed what the reply did.
