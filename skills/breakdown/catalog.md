highlights:
  en:
    - "Enumerate before evaluating: the do-nothing case and the awkward one nobody wants are on the list too, and anything left out is listed as excluded with a reason"
    - "Every claim is tagged fact (verified, and where) or inference (reasoned, and from what) — the two are never blurred"
    - "Uniform depth across cases, because a thin section next to a thick one is a decision made on your behalf"
    - "Phase 2 asks only what changes the outcome, then stops — no answering its own questions, no edits made while you're still deciding"
    - "Phase 3 accounts for every case from phase 1, so nothing quietly disappears between the layout and the recommendation"
